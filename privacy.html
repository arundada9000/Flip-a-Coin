Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Coin Toss Simulator at easyflipping.netlify.app handles information. The site is a free tool built and maintained by Arun Neupane. The short version: everything the tool does happens in your own browser, and we do not collect or store your personal data on any server.

What happens in your browser

  • Coin flips: every toss is generated locally by your browser's random number generator. Results, streaks, and counters are never uploaded anywhere and reset when you refresh the page.
  • Sounds and animations: played locally; no microphone or camera access is ever requested.
  • Offline app: if you install the site as an app, your browser caches the site files on your device so it works offline. No personal data is involved.

Cookies and local storage

The simulator itself does not set tracking cookies. However, third parties described below may set cookies when ads are shown.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • For more on how Google uses data from partner sites, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

Children's privacy

This is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.
